Today you put everything together in a simulated shift. You work in a group of three: Carer 1, Carer 2 and the Client (who becomes the Supervisor and Safety Officer). You help Ren with his morning care: getting ready, a shower using the hoist, grooming and a shave, his medication, and a report to the supervisor. Read this page before your turn.

1 Ren's story **

Read Ren's story first. Everything you do today comes from his plan and his choices.

Your client today

Ren Simpson, 75

  • Ren lives at home with his wife, Suelin.
  • He has Parkinson's disease. It affects how well his hands work, his balance and his energy. He moves and talks slowly and tires easily.
  • He likes to do as much as he can himself.
  • This morning is an "off" morning: Ren is very stiff, so you use a non-weight bearing hoist to move him between the bed and the shower chair.
  • He likes dark, smart-casual clothes. He does not wear white, black, purple or green.
  • The mannequin is Ren for all lifting, moving, showering and shaving. The Client plays Ren when you talk to him.

2 Your team, the golden rules and the equipment **

Each group has three students. You are marked one by one, so both carers do every skill.

Golden rules: these apply to every part of the shift.
Listen to the explanation
  1. NP17 S2 P1Wash your hands before and after every task.
  2. NP17 S2 P2Say your names and ask if it is okay (consent) before you touch Ren or start a task.
  3. NP17 S2 P3Tell Ren each step before you do it. Keep checking he is okay.
  4. NP17 S2 P4Check the bed brakes and bed height before any bed or hoist task.
  5. NP17 S2 P5Check the hoist and sling before you lift: weight limit, no tears, battery charged, clear path.
  6. NP17 S2 P6Look for hazards first: wet floor, clutter, cords.
  7. NP17 S2 P7Keep Ren's privacy and dignity: doors, curtains, keep him covered.
  8. NP17 S2 P8Move safely: bend your knees, keep your back straight, do not twist.
  9. NP17 S2 P9Let Ren do what he can. Prompt him. Do not do it all for him.
  10. NP17 S2 P10Use clothes Ren likes.
  11. NP17 S2 P11Equipment: shower and shower chair, non-weight bearing hoist and sling, mannequin, bed, PPE, towels and washers, toiletries, lotion, razor and shaving cream, toothbrush, dirty-linen skip, continence bin, medication chart, pre-packaged medication, pil-bob, medicine cup and water.

Carer 1

Does the LEFT side of Ren's body and face. Works the hoist once. Combs Ren's hair.

Carer 2

Does the RIGHT side of Ren's body and face. Works the hoist once. Brushes Ren's teeth.

Both carers

In Part 4 you swap roles, so you both give the medication. Share the talking evenly.

Client, then Supervisor and Safety Officer

Plays Ren and Suelin when the carers talk. Becomes the Supervisor and Safety Officer when the carers use the mannequin. Holds the checklist. Can call "STOP" if something is unsafe.

3 How the day works

There are 4 parts of 20 minutes each. Every group does them in the same order: Part 1, 2, 3, then 4. Only 3 or 4 groups are in the practical lab at a time. Your teacher tells you when to start.

While you are NOT in the practical lab, you stay in class and complete ALL the theory, questions and forms in your AT2 book. Before or after your lab turn, you write your OWN progress note for Ren Simpson (Aged Care) and sign the Medication Chart in your OWN AT2 book. Then upload your book.

5 Part 1 PREP and Part 2 WET **

Part 1 PREP (20 min): you arrive at Ren and Suelin's home at the start of the morning shift. Part 2 WET (20 min): the mannequin is Ren. Carer 1 does the LEFT side, Carer 2 the RIGHT side.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP17 S5 P1PREP: read Ren's plan (the hoist, the shower chair, his personal, cultural and social needs). Ask the supervisor if the plan is clear and safe. Wash your hands.
  2. NP17 S5 P2PREP: say hello and both your names, ask if it is okay to help and how much help he wants. Ask about his clothes.
  3. NP17 S5 P3PREP: go to Suelin. She waits for you to come to her. Listen to her news and thank her. Remember it for the report.
  4. NP17 S5 P4PREP: talk with Ren about his cards. Suggest something to help him hold his cards, and a local group he could join.
  5. NP17 S5 P5WET: hands, gloves and apron. Get the equipment. Ask about clothes, soap and the room (warm enough?). Close the doors and curtains.
  6. NP17 S5 P6WET: bed at a safe height, brakes ON. Check the hoist and sling.
  7. NP17 S5 P7WET: undress the bottom half in bed, each carer their own side. Keep Ren covered.
  8. NP17 S5 P8WET: one carer works the hoist to the shower chair, the other looks after Ren. Undress the top half in the chair.
  9. NP17 S5 P9WET: check the water, then ask Ren to check it. Ren washes his face himself. Each carer washes their own side. Ask about his hair. Rinse, taps off.
  10. NP17 S5 P10WET: each carer pats their side dry, lotion and deodorant if he wants. Dress the top half in the chair.
  11. NP17 S5 P11WET: the other carer hoists Ren back to bed. Dress the bottom half in bed. Clean up and wash your hands.

6 Part 3 GROOM: hair, teeth and shave **

Ren is showered, dried and dressed, and sitting comfortably. The mannequin is Ren.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP17 S6 P1Hands, gloves and apron. Doors and curtains closed.
  2. NP17 S6 P2Carer 1 combs Ren's hair. Ask how he likes it.
  3. NP17 S6 P3Carer 2 brushes Ren's teeth: soft, small toothbrush, gentle circles, along the gum line. Sunscreen or accessories if he wants.
  4. NP17 S6 P4Ask Ren if it is okay to shave him. Sit him up, towel on his chest. Wet his face with a warm washer, then shaving cream.
  5. NP17 S6 P5Carer 1 shaves the LEFT side of his face; Carer 2 the RIGHT side. Keep the skin tight, shave the way the hair grows, rinse the razor often.
  6. NP17 S6 P6Rinse his face and pat it dry. Aftershave if he wants. Help him sit comfortably, clean up, gloves off, wash your hands.

7 Part 4 MED: both carers give the medication **

Ren is dressed and comfortable. The Client plays Ren, so talk to him. You do the medication twice: first Carer 1 leads and Carer 2 helps, then you SWAP.

Each student signs the Medication Chart in their OWN AT2 book for the dose they gave.
Listen to the explanation
  1. NP17 S7 P1Ask the supervisor if you can give the medication.
  2. NP17 S7 P2Get the medication chart, blister pack, pil-bob, hand gel, medicine cup and water. Wash your hands.
  3. NP17 S7 P3Say your name, ask Ren if it is okay to help, and how much help he wants.
  4. NP17 S7 P4Do the Rights: the chart matches the pack.
  5. NP17 S7 P5Check the number of pills for this time slot, check it is Ren (name and photo), and check how Ren looks and acts before the dose.
  6. NP17 S7 P6Pop the pills out with the pil-bob. Count them twice. Help Ren take them the right way and watch him swallow.
  7. NP17 S7 P7Check Ren after the dose. Clean up, put the pack and chart away, wash your hands.
  8. NP17 S7 P8If anything does not match, stop and tell the supervisor. Ren can say no.
  9. NP17 S7 P9Then SWAP: the other carer leads.

8 Part 4 REPORT: tell the supervisor, then write your own note **

After the medication, both carers give the verbal report to the supervisor in the lab. Then, in class, each student writes their own progress note.

Write your OWN progress note for Ren Simpson (Aged Care) in your OWN AT2 book, in class, before or after your lab turn. Do not copy another student's note. Then upload your book.
Listen to the explanation
  1. NP17 S8 P1Tell the supervisor what care you gave and how Ren was.
  2. NP17 S8 P2Tell the supervisor what Suelin told you, and what needs to happen next.
  3. NP17 S8 P3Tell the supervisor about any problem Ren told you about during your shift, and suggest a fix.
  4. NP17 S8 P4Tell the supervisor your two ideas for Ren (from Part 1).
  5. NP17 S8 P5Keep Ren's information private: only tell the supervisor, and write it in your notes.
  6. NP17 S8 P6Your progress note (in your OWN AT2 book): the date, 24-hour time, what happened, facts only, your name and signature.

9 What students often miss **

Your teacher has watched many groups do this shift. These are the things that are missed most often. Check yourself.

The rules of the assessment: you are marked one by one, so you must do every skill yourself. Follow the plan and the KAMA procedures. If something is unsafe, the Supervisor and Safety Officer calls "STOP". Stop first, talk after.
Listen to the explanation
  1. NP17 S9 P1Asking the supervisor if the plan is clear and safe before starting.
  2. NP17 S9 P2Brakes ON and the bed height set before any bed or hoist task.
  3. NP17 S9 P3Checking the hoist and sling out loud before lifting.
  4. NP17 S9 P4Asking for consent before every task, not only at the start.
  5. NP17 S9 P5Letting Ren wash his own face and do what he can.
  6. NP17 S9 P6Checking the water temperature first, then asking Ren to check.
  7. NP17 S9 P7Keeping Ren covered the whole time.
  8. NP17 S9 P8Both carers doing their own side, and both working the hoist.
  9. NP17 S9 P9Counting the pills twice and watching Ren swallow.
  10. NP17 S9 P10Going to Suelin, and passing on her news in the report.
  11. NP17 S9 P11Washing hands at the end of every part.
