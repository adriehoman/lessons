1 The day at a glance and the safety rules

Four skills today, in this order: the bed, the slide sheet and slide board, the Sara Steady, then the car.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP13 S1 P1Hand hygiene before and after every task.
  2. NP13 S1 P2Introduce yourself, explain the task and get consent. Keep checking consent as you go.
  3. NP13 S1 P3Privacy and dignity: close the door and curtains, keep the person covered.
  4. NP13 S1 P4Check the care plan first: special needs, equipment, how many staff.
  5. NP13 S1 P5Check the area for hazards, and move personal items out of the way.
  6. NP13 S1 P6Bed: brakes on, bed at your waist height. Put it back down at the end.
  7. NP13 S1 P7Work with your partner and talk to each other: "Ready, steady, slide."
  8. NP13 S1 P8Good body mechanics: feet apart, bend your knees, keep your back straight, no twisting.
  9. NP13 S1 P9At the end: make the person comfortable, return the call bell and personal items, put equipment away, clean your hands.

2 Task 2: change the linen on an occupied bed

The person stays in bed while you and your partner change the sheets. Two workers, one on each side.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP13 S2 P1Get ready: check the care plan, get consent, clean your hands, collect the linen and put it on a clean surface. Close the door and curtains, put on PPE, check for hazards. Brakes on, bed up.
  2. NP13 S2 P2Strip the top: remove extra pillows and pillowcases, fold the quilt and blanket onto a clean surface. Put the top sheet straight into the linen skip.
  3. NP13 S2 P3Cover the person with a towel for dignity. Help them roll onto their side, facing one worker.
  4. NP13 S2 P4First side: the other worker loosens the bottom sheet and rolls it to the middle. Put on the clean bottom sheet, mitre the corners, and roll the rest to the middle.
  5. NP13 S2 P5Roll the person over the roll to the other side. The other worker takes off the dirty sheet straight into the skip (never on the floor), then pulls the clean sheet through and mitres the corners.
  6. NP13 S2 P6Finish: remove the dignity towel, put on the top sheet, blanket and quilt with mitred corners, leave room for the toes, do not cover the face. New pillowcases on.
  7. NP13 S2 P7Make the person comfortable, call bell and items back, bed down, equipment away, PPE off, clean your hands.

Watch: Occupied bed making (SacMed Training)

Watch out Dirty linen goes straight into the skip. Never put it on the floor or shake it.

3 Task 7: slide sheet and slide board

A slide sheet lets you move a person in bed with very little effort. You never drag a person on the bottom sheet. A slide board helps a person slide sideways between two surfaces.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP13 S3 P1Get ready: check the plan, clean your hands, consent, partner on the opposite side, equipment ready, door and curtains closed, items out of the bed. Brakes on, bed up.
  2. NP13 S3 P2Put the slide sheet in: use small log rolls (roll the person to one side, then the other) to slide the sheet under them. Keep the person covered.
  3. NP13 S3 P3Move to the side: the person folds their arms across their chest. Hands at the shoulder and hip. One worker pushes with open hands, the other pulls the top layer. Talk: "Ready, steady, slide."
  4. NP13 S3 P4Move up the bed: a pillow at the head of the bed for protection. The person folds their arms, tucks their chin, bends their knees with feet on the mattress. Both workers gather the top layer near the shoulders and slide together.
  5. NP13 S3 P5Take it out: pull the bottom layer of the slide sheet out from under the person.
  6. NP13 S3 P6Slide board: used for a sideways move between two surfaces at the same height, for example bed to wheelchair. Brakes on, armrest off, board firmly under the person's thigh, the person leans away and slides in small steps. Follow the care plan and your teacher's demonstration.
  7. NP13 S3 P7Make the person comfortable, call bell and items back, store the slide sheet and board, clean your hands.

4 Task 8: the weight bearing hoist (Sara Steady)

A weight bearing hoist (the Sara Steady) helps a person who can take some weight on their legs to stand and move from A to B. Always two workers.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP13 S4 P1Check first: the care plan (is this the right hoist and sling?), the weight limits of the hoist and sling, the sling has no frayed edges, the battery is charged, and there is a clear path from A to B.
  2. NP13 S4 P2Clean your hands, introduce yourself and get consent.
  3. NP13 S4 P3Sling on: the person sits forward. Put the sling around their back, with the sash belt firm around the hips, as the maker's instructions say.
  4. NP13 S4 P4Feet on the platform, knees against the knee pad. Attach the sling to the hoist safely.
  5. NP13 S4 P5Lift: the person holds on. Lift with a smooth, steady movement. Keep checking the sling, the knees and the hands as they come to a stand.
  6. NP13 S4 P6Move the hoist slowly to place B, then lower smoothly. Make sure the person is seated properly.
  7. NP13 S4 P7Sling off: disconnect the sling, help the person lift their feet off the platform, undo the sash belt and remove the sling.
  8. NP13 S4 P8Talk the person through every step. Make them comfortable, put the hoist and sling away, clean your hands.

5 Task 6A: into and out of a car

You help a mobile person (they can walk) get into and out of a car safely. The rule: bottom first, then the legs.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP13 S5 P1Get ready: clean your hands, introduce yourself, get consent, and get any equipment (for example a cushion or a car handle). Move the seat back if needed.
  2. NP13 S5 P2Into the car: open the door. The person walks to the door and holds the door frame or car handle for support.
  3. NP13 S5 P3The person turns so their back is to the seat, leans forward a little, and lowers their bottom onto the seat. Watch their head on the door frame.
  4. NP13 S5 P4Then one leg in, then the other leg in. Help them get comfortable and put on the seat belt.
  5. NP13 S5 P5Out of the car: open the door, undo the seat belt. The person turns and puts one leg out, then the other leg out, with feet flat on the ground.
  6. NP13 S5 P6The person leans forward and stands up, using the door frame or handle. Check they are steady before they walk, and give them their walking aid if they use one. Clean your hands.
  7. NP13 S5 P7From a wheelchair (what we practise today): park the wheelchair close to the car seat, at a small angle, and put the brakes on. Swing the footplates away and the person puts their feet flat on the ground. They stand, turn, and sit down bottom first, then bring their legs in. Getting out is the opposite, with the wheelchair brakes on before they sit back down.

6 What students often miss

Your assessor looks at every step. These are the steps people forget most often.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP13 S6 P1Hand hygiene at the end of the task, not only at the start.
  2. NP13 S6 P2Consent at the start, and explaining each step as you go.
  3. NP13 S6 P3Brakes on and bed height up before you start, and back down at the end.
  4. NP13 S6 P4Call bell and personal items back within reach.
  5. NP13 S6 P5Saying the hoist checks (weight limit, sling, battery, path) out loud so your assessor hears them.
  6. NP13 S6 P6Putting equipment away (slide sheet, slide board, hoist and sling).
  7. NP13 S6 P7Talking with your partner: "Ready, steady, slide."
