On Wednesday you will work a morning shift at Lakeview Respite Care. Your client is Mary Jones. Today your teacher shows you every part of the shift, in the same order you will do it. Follow this page during the demonstration. On Wednesday you practise in your group first, and then your teacher watches you when you are the Main Carer.

1 What is the simulated shift?

A simulated shift is a practice shift in a pretend workplace. It looks and feels like a real morning on placement.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S1 P1You will work one morning shift with Mary Jones: personal care, moving Mary safely, housekeeping, and a difficult conversation.
  2. NP16 S1 P2It is not acting. The goal is to show safe work, respectful talking and good judgement, like a real support worker.
  3. NP16 S1 P3Your teacher assesses you when you are the Main Carer. The other roles help the shift run safely.
  4. NP16 S1 P4Everything your teacher marks on Wednesday is shown today, is in your handout, and you practise it in your group first.
  5. NP16 S1 P5Today: the teacher shows each part, you try some steps, and you get your group, role and handouts for Wednesday.

Your three handouts: read them and prepare before Wednesday

  • Main Carer instructions: every step of the shift, and words you could say. Everyone reads this.
  • Client script (Mary): for the student who plays Mary. When it is your turn as the Main Carer, the conversation should feel real, so do not practise Mary's lines with the Main Carer.
  • Supervisor checklist: the Supervisor ticks each step and gives feedback after each round.

2 Meet Mary Jones

Read Mary's story before you start. Use it to guide what you do and what you say.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S2 P1Mary is 80. She normally lives on her own at 21 Lake Drive, and she is proud that she manages by herself.
  2. NP16 S2 P2Two weeks ago she had a fall at home. She is staying at Lakeview Respite Care to get stronger. She goes home on Friday.
  3. NP16 S2 P3She walks short distances with a 4-wheel walker. She needs help to move in bed and to transfer (move from one seat to another).
  4. NP16 S2 P4She can make simple meals and she loves a chat, especially about her daughter and grandchildren. They have been visiting more lately.
  5. NP16 S2 P5Her care plan says: slide sheet with two carers, supervised transfers, 4-wheel walker for walking.

3 The four roles and how you rotate

You work in a group of 4. Everyone gets a turn as the Main Carer.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S3 P1Main Carer: leads the shift and does all the talking with Mary and the Supervisor. Calls the Supervisor and writes the incident report. Speak in your own words: no script.
  2. NP16 S3 P2Second Carer: helps with the two-person tasks (slide sheet and bed linen) and stays close during transfers. Follows the Main Carer. Does not take over the talking.
  3. NP16 S3 P3Client (Mary): plays Mary with the client script. Only moves when the carers ask, the way they ask.
  4. NP16 S3 P4Supervisor: watches with the checklist, answers the phone call, and is the safety officer. The Supervisor can call "STOP" at any time if something is unsafe. Anyone can call STOP for safety. No questions, no penalty.
  5. NP16 S3 P5Round 1: do all 7 parts. Then swap roles: the Second Carer becomes the Main Carer.
  6. NP16 S3 P6Round 2: do all the parts except the linen change. After the debrief, the two Main Carers go back to class to finish their assessment, and two new students join the group as Mary and the Supervisor.

4 Golden rules for every task

These rules count in every part of the shift. Your teacher watches for them from the moment you arrive.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S4 P1Dress like placement: professional clothes, closed-in shoes, no jewellery, hair tied back. Be on time. Bring your phone.
  2. NP16 S4 P2Clean your hands before and after every task.
  3. NP16 S4 P3Introduce yourself and ask for consent before you touch Mary or start a task. Then wait for her yes.
  4. NP16 S4 P4Explain each step before you do it, and keep checking Mary is okay.
  5. NP16 S4 P5Check the bed brakes and the bed height before any bed task. Look for hazards first.
  6. NP16 S4 P6Protect Mary's privacy and dignity: close the door and curtains, keep her covered.
  7. NP16 S4 P7Good body position: bed at hip height, back straight, knees bent, stand close, never twist.
  8. NP16 S4 P8Prompt Mary to move herself. Never lift her.
Watch out Consent is a conversation, not an announcement. Ask, then wait for the answer.

5 Parts 1 and 2: start of shift and the slide sheet

Mary has slipped down the bed after breakfast. She is not comfortable. You and the Second Carer move her up the bed with a slide sheet.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S5 P1Part 1: read the care plan, clean your hands, and check the room for hazards. Tell the Supervisor about anything you find.
  2. NP16 S5 P2Part 2: clean your hands, introduce yourself, explain, and ask for consent.
  3. NP16 S5 P3Get the slide sheets. Close the door and curtains. Check the bed for personal items (glasses, call bell, tissues).
  4. NP16 S5 P4Bed to hip height. Brakes on. Carers stand on opposite sides.
  5. NP16 S5 P5Log roll Mary to put the slide sheet under her. Keep her covered.
  6. NP16 S5 P6Mary folds her arms, tucks her chin, bends her knees. Put a pillow against the bed head.
  7. NP16 S5 P7Both carers hold the top sheet near her shoulders. "Ready, steady, slide."
  8. NP16 S5 P8Take the slide sheet out by the bottom layer. Give back the call bell. Bed back to the lowest height. Clean your hands.
Watch out Talk to both the Second Carer and Mary at every step.

6 Part 3: from the bed to the wheelchair

It is time for morning tea in the lounge. You help Mary move from the bed into the wheelchair.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S6 P1Clean your hands, explain and ask for consent.
  2. NP16 S6 P2Bed height to match the wheelchair. Bed brakes on.
  3. NP16 S6 P3Help Mary sit on the side of the bed, feet flat on the floor. Wait a moment: she may feel dizzy.
  4. NP16 S6 P4Wheelchair next to the bed, footplates out of the way, brakes on.
  5. NP16 S6 P5Prompt Mary to stand: hands on the bed, lean forward, rock 1, 2, 3, push up with her legs.
  6. NP16 S6 P6Her hand closest to the wheelchair goes to the far armrest. She steps and turns, the other hand goes to the other armrest, and she lowers slowly.
  7. NP16 S6 P7Footplates back, feet on the footplates. Clean your hands. The Second Carer takes Mary to the lounge.

7 Part 4: change the linen on the empty bed

Mary is in the lounge. You and the Second Carer change her bed linen.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S7 P1Clean your hands and put on gloves and an apron (PPE).
  2. NP16 S7 P2Put the clean linen on a clean surface. Never on the floor or a used chair.
  3. NP16 S7 P3Check for hazards and Mary's belongings. Bed height up, brakes on.
  4. NP16 S7 P4Take the linen off piece by piece. No shaking, and do not hold it against your uniform.
  5. NP16 S7 P5Dirty linen goes straight into the linen skip.
  6. NP16 S7 P6Take off your PPE and clean your hands before you touch the clean linen.
  7. NP16 S7 P7Bottom sheet tight, with a mitre fold (hospital corner) at each corner. Leave room for Mary's toes in the top layers.
  8. NP16 S7 P8Put the furniture and Mary's things back. Bed to the lowest height. Clean your hands.

Watch out Shaking linen spreads germs into the air.

8 Parts 5 and 6: stand, walk and sit

Morning tea is finished. The physio wants Mary to walk a little with her walker, back to her room, and then sit in her armchair.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S8 P1Stand: clean your hands, ask for consent. Mary shuffles forward until her feet are flat on the floor.
  2. NP16 S8 P2Hands on the arms of the chair, lean forward ("nose over toes"), rock 1, 2, 3, and push up with her legs.
  3. NP16 S8 P3Give only the help she needs. Your hands are ready, not gripping.
  4. NP16 S8 P4Put the walker in front of her after she stands. Check she is steady before she walks. Walk beside her.
  5. NP16 S8 P5Sit: ask for consent. Mary stands with the backs of her legs touching the seat.
  6. NP16 S8 P6One hand down to the arm of the chair, then the other hand, and she lowers slowly. Help her get comfortable. Clean your hands.

9 Part 7: a cup of tea and a chat

You make Mary a cup of tea and sit with her for a chat. Today Mary is not her usual happy self. Listen carefully.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S9 P1Talk about Mary: going home on Friday, her family, how she feels. Keep the chat about her, not you.
  2. NP16 S9 P2Notice the signs: Mary is quiet and flat. Her favourite crystal vase is gone. She says she will not need groceries for going home. She has not been eating much. Her medicine script is on the table, not filled.
  3. NP16 S9 P3Ask gently: "Mary, you don't seem your usual happy self today. Is something worrying you?"
  4. NP16 S9 P4Mary cries and tells you her daughter asked for her debit card to pay some bills, and now there is not enough money until the next pension day.
  5. NP16 S9 P5Stay calm and listen. "Thank you for telling me, Mary. That sounds really worrying, and it's not your fault."
  6. NP16 S9 P6Do not judge her daughter. Do not promise to keep a secret. Do not tell Mary what to do.
Watch out Never say "I won't tell anyone". You must tell your supervisor, so that promise traps you.

10 Part 7: the phone call to your supervisor

Mary has told you something worrying. Follow the steps.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S10 P1Ask for consent: "I can't keep this just between us, because I want you to be safe. Is it okay if I talk to my supervisor?" Wait for her answer.
  2. NP16 S10 P2Go to a private place, where nobody can hear you, and phone your supervisor.
  3. NP16 S10 P3Say who you are and where you are: "Hello, this is ..., I'm with Mary Jones in room 4."
  4. NP16 S10 P4Report the facts: what you saw and what Mary said, and that she gave consent to tell you.
  5. NP16 S10 P5Ask: "What would you like me to do next?" This is the limit of your role: you report and refer. You do not investigate, you do not ring the daughter, you do not fix it yourself.
  6. NP16 S10 P6Go back to Mary, reassure her, and make sure she is comfortable and safe.

11 Part 7: the incident report, facts only

After the call, you complete the incident report. Your teacher checks every section.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S11 P1Section 1, reporter details: the organisation (Lakeview Respite Care), your name, phone number, position (support worker) and service location.
  2. NP16 S11 P2Section 2, incident details: the date and time, when you were first told, and the place.
  3. NP16 S11 P3Section 3, who was involved: Mary's details, and the staff involved, and whether each person is a participant, witness or victim.
  4. NP16 S11 P4Section 4, what happened: what happened and what you did. Facts only. Use Mary's own words in quotation marks. Say who you reported to and when.
  5. NP16 S11 P5Good: "At 10:20 am Mary said, 'My daughter asked for my debit card to pay some bills and now there's not enough money until next pension day.'" Not good: "Her daughter is stealing from her."
  6. NP16 S11 P6Privacy: talk about it only with your supervisor. Only the supervisor sees the report, and it is stored safely. Do not talk about it with other students or staff.
Watch out In 2026, the incident report was the main reason students had to try again: opinions instead of facts, missing details in sections 2 and 3, the witness part, and too little detail in section 4.

12 After the shift: questions and handing in

Your assessment is not finished until the shift, the incident report and the questions are all done.

Listen to the explanation
  1. NP16 S12 P1After the shift your teacher asks you some questions: how you notice your own stress (the signs), what you do to manage it, who you talk to at work, and how you protected Mary's privacy. (Look back at Lesson 6.)
  2. NP16 S12 P2Hand in the incident report with no empty boxes.
  3. NP16 S12 P3Answer every question. Use clear, simple, factual answers.
  4. NP16 S12 P4Check names, dates and the observation date, and tick the checkboxes.
  5. NP16 S12 P5Before Wednesday: read the Main Carer instructions and the Supervisor checklist in the class OneNote, and practise the words you could say.
  6. NP16 S12 P6Wednesday: dress like placement, bring your phone, be on time. If the abuse topic is hard for you, talk to your teacher before Wednesday.
